About Swarnadipa (SRT)

PT. Swarnadipa Rampai Technologi or commonly named as SRT, is part of Sriwjaya Teknik Group, which has well-known from its debuted as Velcro Fasteners distributor in Indonesia. The Company has just established on June 2025 and currently have supplied to big projects in Indonesia for passive network infrastructure & IP ELV solutions. We run our daily business in project and retail market and develop our IP&E online business for supplying electronic & electrical components to the specific industries in Manufacturing, Aviation Maintenance, Oil & Gas & Mining and Building Maintenance, through our golden-pots.com website.

By maintaining a good name and through the principles of caution and seriousness in building a brand. We have been trusted to become Representative Agency for some global brands, and we support them also to expand their market in Indonesia, by helping them in Sales, Marketing, and Product Development.
